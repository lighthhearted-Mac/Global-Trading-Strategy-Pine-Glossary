# Trading Strategy Repository — Master Index

Built on 2026-11-03.

## 01 — Massive Repositories
- **Papers With Backtest**: 5,000+ backtested strategies from 30 years of research.
- **The Quant-Trading-Vault**: 5,806 strategy specifications (Pine Script, JS, Python, C++).
- **Composer Symphony Database**: Thousands of daily-updated, fully backtested ETF/stock strategies.

## 02 — Research Databases
- **SSRN**: Tens of thousands of finance papers, free.
- **151 Trading Strategies**: The foundational 480-page reference (SSRN abstract 3247865).
- **arXiv q-fin**: Preprints in quantitative finance.

## 03 — Platform Libraries
- **QuantConnect LEAN**: Open-source algorithmic trading engine + strategy library.
- **StockSharp**: C# and Python ready-made strategies, runnable in Designer/Runner/Shell.

## 04 — Graveyards (Failed Strategies)
- **Trading Strategy Graveyard (GitHub)**: 35 pre-registered backtests, 29 killed.
- **Zenodo Graveyard**: Forward-running ledger of killed hypotheses.
- **PRUVIQ / StratProof**: Services that publish their losers, including retired presets and OOS failures.

## 05 — Books & Foundational Papers
- **151 Trading Strategies**: Free English PDF on SSRN; GitHub mirror available.
- **Awesome Systematic Trading (Books section)**: Curated reading list.

## 06 — Curated Awesome Lists
- **paperswithbacktest/awesome-systematic-trading**: 136+ libraries, 4,843 backtested papers, 55 books, 22 videos.
- **wangzhe3224/awesome-systematic-trading**: Libraries, packages, and resources across all asset classes.
- **Composer Atlas**: Community-curated database of Composer symphonies.

## Honest Caveats
1. **Most strategies do not work.** The graveyards exist because the majority of ideas fail after friction and out-of-sample testing.
2. **These are idea repositories, not profit guarantees.** Use them for research, hypothesis generation, and benchmarking.
3. **Backtest overfitting is the default failure mode.** Always run out-of-sample and paper-trade before committing capital.
